Hacker Cottage Weekend · Merch · Round 1

Hat concepts

Twenty-one embroidery designs for the tan dad cap, plus tumbler adaptations. Each is shown on the fabric color it would stitch onto. Every design respects real embroidery limits: bold shapes, satin-stitch friendly text, and one to three thread colors. Final artwork gets digitized by the embroiderer.

Stitch area
Cap front: 100 × 55 mm max. Back arch: 90 × 35 mm. Side: 50 × 30 mm.
Text floor
5 mm letter height minimum. Strokes 1 mm or thicker.
Thread palette
espresso amber rust cream
Rule of thumb
Fewer colors reads sharper on fabric and costs less per unit.
